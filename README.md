# AquaHarvX Terms & Policy Portal

Official Terms of Service, Conditions, and Privacy Policy for the **AquaHarvX** mobile application, operated by **SIDDHIE ARUMUGAA TRADERS**.

## Included Pages
- **`index.html`**: Legal & Compliance Portal hub
- **`privacy.html`**: Privacy Policy (compliant with Google Play Store Developer Policies & India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)
- **`terms.html`**: Terms and Conditions & Terms of Service (including mandatory third-party developer non-liability clause)

## Free Hosting via GitHub Pages

To make these pages live at a public HTTPS URL (required by Google Play Store):

1. Go to this repository on GitHub: `https://github.com/aquaharvx-arch/AquaHarvX_Terms_and_Policy`
2. Click **Settings** (tab at the top).
3. In the left sidebar, click **Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment**:
   - **Source:** Deploy from a branch
   - **Branch:** Select `main` and `/ (root)`
   - Click **Save**.
5. Within 1 minute, GitHub will give you your live HTTPS URLs:
   - **Privacy Policy:** `https://aquaharvx-arch.github.io/AquaHarvX_Terms_and_Policy/privacy.html`
   - **Terms & Conditions:** `https://aquaharvx-arch.github.io/AquaHarvX_Terms_and_Policy/terms.html`
   - **Portal Home:** `https://aquaharvx-arch.github.io/AquaHarvX_Terms_and_Policy/`

*(Optional: You can also configure a custom domain like `terms.aquaharvx.in` or `aquaharvx.in` under the Custom Domain setting in GitHub Pages).*

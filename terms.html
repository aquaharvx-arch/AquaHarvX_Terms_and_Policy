1. Intermediary Status & Direct-Connection Model (IT Act § 79)

1.1. Discovery Directory Only: AquaHarvX (the "Platform") is owned and operated by Siddhie Arumugaa Traders ("SAT", "Firm", "We", "Us"), a sole proprietorship based in Andhra Pradesh, India. The Platform functions strictly as a technology-enabled telecommunications directory facilitating peer-to-peer discovery between independent shrimp farmers ("Sellers"), third-party wholesale procurement agents/factories ("Third-Party Buyers"), and SAT.

1.2. Complete Non-Involvement in Settlements: AquaHarvX does not operate, manage, handle, or facilitate any monetary payments, financial escrow, clearing-house operations, or financial custody. Sellers and Buyers communicate directly through standard telephonic channels. 1.3. Section 79 Safe Harbor: The Platform qualifies as an "Intermediary" under Section 2(1)(w) of the Information Technology Act, 2000.

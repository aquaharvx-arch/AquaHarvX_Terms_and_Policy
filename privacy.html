Privacy Policy

AquaHarvX is committed to protecting your personal information and privacy. This Privacy Policy details our data practices in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India), Google Play Developer Policies, and applicable privacy regulations.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal data strictly necessary to facilitate prawn marketplace transactions:

  • Phone Number: Your 10-digit mobile number, verified via One-Time Password (OTP) sent by SMS. Used as your primary account identifier and to enable direct calling between verified buyers and sellers.
  • Personal Identity: Your full name and optional business/trader firm name, displayed on harvest listings or buyer offers to establish trading trust.
  • Geographical & Farm Location: State, District, Mandal, and Village details. This is required because shrimp harvests and daily spot prices are geographically localized to ensure trade matching.
  • Prawn Harvest & Trading Details: Prawn count sizes (20 Count to 400 Count), tonnage available/sought, and indicative offer prices.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Data:
    • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to deliver transactional alerts and market updates;
    • Firebase Crashlytics crash reports, error stack traces, and anonymous device diagnostics to fix software bugs.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is collected and processed for the following specified legitimate purposes:

  1. Authentication & Security: To sign you in securely using passwordless SMS OTPs without storing passwords.
  2. Marketplace Listing & Matching: To display seller prawn harvest listings to interested buyers, and buyer "Offer More" quotes to farmers.
  3. Communication Between Traders: To allow sellers to contact prospective buyers directly via phone when interested in an offer.
  4. Notifications: To deliver alerts when market prices update, an offer is outbid, or an offer is about to expire.
  5. Regulatory & Legal Compliance: To comply with Indian statutory requirements, including electronic consent logging under the DPDP Act 2023.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted industry-standard infrastructure providers who process data strictly under confidentiality agreements:

  • MSG91: For delivering transactional SMS OTPs and authentication codes to your mobile phone.
  • Google Firebase: For real-time push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and stability crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Secure cloud application and database servers hosting backend APIs with encrypted storage.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to any marketing or advertisement brokers.

5. Data Security and Encryption

We implement comprehensive security measures to safeguard your information:

  • All network communications between the mobile app and server use HTTPS with Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+).
  • Authentication utilizes cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with rate-limiting protection.
  • Access to backend databases is strictly restricted to authorized administrative personnel.

6. Account and Data Deletion Rights

In accordance with Section 12 of the DPDP Act 2023 and Google Play requirements, you have the right to withdraw your consent and have your personal data permanently deleted.

How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can delete your account at any time directly inside the AquaHarvX application:

  1. Open the AquaHarvX app;
  2. Tap on the Profile icon at the top-left of the Home screen;
  3. Scroll down and select Delete Account;
  4. Confirm deletion.

What happens upon deletion: Your user profile, active listings, and live buyer offers will be immediately purged from our active systems. You may also request deletion by emailing our Grievance Officer at aquaharvx@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

AquaHarvX is strictly a commercial agricultural trade platform designed for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we discover that a minor under 18 has registered, their account will be promptly removed.

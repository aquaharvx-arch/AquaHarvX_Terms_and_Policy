Privacy Policy

Data Fiduciary: Siddhie Arumugaa Traders Jurisdiction: Andhra Pradesh, India Effective Date: October 2026

1. Scope, Commitment & Governing Principles

This Privacy Policy describes how Siddhie Arumugaa Traders ("SAT", "AquaHarvX", "we", "us") collects, stores, processes, and protects personal data obtained through the AquaHarvX mobile application. We operate in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and Google Play & Apple Store privacy standards.

🛡️ Non-Sale of Personal Data: Siddhie Arumugaa Traders never sells, leases, or trades user telephone numbers or location data to third-party telemarketers, credit agencies, or advertising networks.

2. Information We Collect & Method of Collection

We collect only the minimum information strictly necessary to operate the platform and connect farmers with buyers:

  • 2.1. Account Identification: Mobile telephone number (verified via One-Time Password / OTP), chosen role (Farmer / Buyer), and optional business/trade name.
  • 2.2. Geographic Location Data: Self-selected administrative location parameters (State: Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, Tamil Nadu, Kerala; District; Mandal; Village / landmark) required to calculate geographic proximity for harvest logistics. The app does not request or track continuous background GPS location.
  • 2.3. Trade Listing Attributes: Approximate shrimp count range (e.g., 20C to 400C), anticipated lot tonnage, expected harvest date/time, and indicative buying bid premiums (+₹/kg).
  • 2.4. Technical Log Data: Device model, operating system version, IP address, and application interaction timestamps stored solely for fraud monitoring and crash diagnostics.

3. Purpose of Data Processing & Public Visibility

3.1. Telecommunications Bridge: Your telephone number is collected strictly to enable direct telephonic voice calls between prospective buyers and sellers when an authenticated user taps "CALL BUYER" or "CALL SELLER".

3.2. PRN Anonymization: General listings display only the user's Public Reference Number ("PRN") and broad district/mandal location to preserve privacy. Full telephone numbers are only accessed when an authenticated user initiates a direct contact inquiry.

4. Data Security, Retention & Third-Party Services

4.1. Storage & Encryption: All account data is stored on secure cloud database servers with transit encryption (TLS 1.3) and restricted operational access controls.

4.2. Third-Party Infrastructure: We utilize trusted telecom and cloud infrastructure vendors (e.g., SMS OTP gateway providers, Google Firebase, or AWS) strictly to deliver technical application services under confidentiality agreements.

4.3. App Store Permission Restraints: The application does not request unnecessary sensitive Android/iOS permissions such as continuous background GPS location, camera roll access, micro-location tracking, or contact book access.

4.4. Statutory Data Retention: Pursuant to Rule 3(1)(h) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, basic user registration records, authentication logs, and communication audit trails are retained for a minimum period of 180 days following account cancellation or deletion.

5. User Data Rights & Contact Information

Users may exercise their statutory data rights at any time:

  • Review & Correction: Review and correct registration data in My Profile;
  • Account & Data Erasure: Permanently delete your account and associated profile data directly inside the application via My Profile > Delete Account & Data. Upon confirmation, public listings, active offers, and user identifiers are permanently removed from public view;
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw platform consent by discontinuing app usage and deleting your profile.
